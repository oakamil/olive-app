Olive Finder

An electronic plate-solving finder and offline sky atlas for your telescope. Mount your phone—or connect an external USB astronomy camera on Android or iPad—to determine where your telescope is pointing with on-device plate-solving.

Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store View Source on GitHub

How It Works

Step 1

Mount or Connect a Camera

Attach your phone to your telescope with a secure mount, or plug in a UVC astronomy camera (SC2210 + 25mm lens recommended on Android and iPad).

Step 2

Align Once

Center a bright star or target in your eyepiece and tap to set the boresight offset between the camera and telescope optical axis.

Step 3

Find & Log Targets

Follow push-to guidance on the built-in sky map, log observations offline, or connect SkySafari or Stellarium over local WiFi.

Key Features

Interactive Sky Map & DSS2

Full-screen star map with optional DSS2 photographic sky imagery, high-resolution object previews, constellation lines, and a live plate-solved camera overlay.

Sky Time Simulation (1900–2050)

Step forward or backward in time or enter any custom date and time with IAU axial precession and DE421/MPC solar system ephemerides.

Offline Astronomical Catalog

Search and filter Messier, NGC, IC, Caldwell, Collinder, Herschel 400, Barnard, Sharpless, Abell, Arp, stars, planets, the Moon, asteroids, and comets.

Observing Lists & Logging

Build custom observing lists, record session logs with notes and equipment details, and import or export CSV files locally.

Hybrid Plate-Solving & IMU

Fuses continuous on-device plate-solving with internal motion sensors for smooth positional updates as you slew the mount, plus live star HFR readouts for focusing.

Planetarium Integration

Emulates a Meade LX200 and ASCOM Alpaca mount over WiFi so companion apps like SkySafari and Stellarium can track your telescope position.

Hardware note: A secure phone or tablet mount / piggyback adapter for your telescope is required when using the built-in camera or motion sensors.
