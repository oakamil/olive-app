# Privacy Policy for Olive Finder

**Effective Date:** June 4, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 23, 2026

Olive Finder is provided by Omair Kamil at no cost and is intended for use "as is".

This page informs users about policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using Olive Finder on Android, iOS, and iPadOS.

If you choose to use Olive Finder, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. Any information accessed by the app is used solely to provide its functionality and is not sold, shared, or transmitted to external servers by the developer.

## Information Access and Local Processing

Olive Finder requests access to device capabilities strictly on-device:

- **Location Data:** Used to convert celestial coordinates (Right Ascension and Declination) into local horizontal directions (Altitude and Azimuth) and maintain telescope alignment. You may also enter coordinates manually in Settings instead of granting GPS access.
- **Camera:** Used only while actively capturing night-sky frames for on-device star plate-solving, live sky-map camera overlays, and boresight/focus calibration. Images are processed locally and are not uploaded.
- **Motion Sensors (IMU):** Used locally to provide smooth, high-framerate orientation updates between plate solves.
- **Local Network (WiFi):** Used to expose standard Meade LX200 and ASCOM Alpaca telescope control servers on your local network so companion planetarium apps (such as SkySafari or Stellarium) can read telescope position.
- **Speech / Microphone (Optional):** Used only if you invoke voice dictation when entering observation notes. Olive Finder does not record or store audio; speech recognition is handled by your device's operating system (Apple or Google), which may process audio on-device or via its own servers depending on your system settings.

## Data Storage and Retention

We are committed to your privacy. All personal and observing data handled by the app is:

- **Processed Locally:** Your location, camera frames, sensor readings, observing lists, and observation logs never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to export/share a CSV file.
- **Stored Locally:** Saved coordinates, alignment settings, observing lists, and observation logs are stored strictly locally on your device.
- **Retained Only as Needed:** Live camera frames and sensor data are processed in memory and not archived. Saved settings and observing data remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.

## Optional Astronomy Data Downloads

If you choose to download optional sky background imagery (Digitized Sky Survey 2 / DSS2 HiPS tiles from CDS Aladin) or update comet orbital elements (from the IAU Minor Planet Center), your device makes direct HTTPS requests to those public scientific data providers. Those servers receive standard network request metadata (such as your IP address); Olive Finder does not attach any personal identifiers, accounts, or analytics to these requests.

## Third-Party Services

Olive Finder does not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or third-party services that collect information used to identify you.

## Security

Because sensitive data (such as location, camera frames, and observation logs) is processed and stored exclusively on your local device without being transmitted to developer servers, it is not exposed to remote server breaches. However, no method of electronic storage on a mobile device is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## European Union (EU) Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Because your data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or third parties, we do not collect, hold, or process your personal data on any servers. All data remains under your control. You may exercise your right to erasure or restrict processing at any time by revoking permissions in your device settings, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

Olive Finder does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Because the app operates locally on-device and does not transmit personal data over the internet, it does not collect or store personal data from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and take effect immediately upon posting.
